What the projects share

The same habits, across every codebase

Every claim traces to a source

Findings link back to the page and text they came from; benchmark figures are read from committed result files rather than retyped.

Refusing beats guessing

Missing evidence produces a typed refusal or a named gap, not a plausible-looking number.

Thresholds fixed in advance

Benchmark tests are written down, and in UMNA hashed, before they run. Failed rounds stay in the record.

Honest about status

Each project says what it does not show. The clinical projects are research prototypes, not medical devices.
